Explainable Network Health Scoring Formula

Health Score = max(0, min(100, 100 - LatencyPenalty - AnomalyPenalty - LossPenalty))
Penalty Component Mathematical Formula Weight / Scale
Latency Penalty max(0, RTT - 50.0) × 0.15 0.15 deduction per ms above 50 ms baseline
Anomaly Penalty 20.0 (if is_anomaly == True) Instant 20-point deduction for Isolation Forest flag
Packet Loss Penalty PacketLossPct × 50.0 Deducts 50 points per 1.0% packet drop
Status Classification Thresholds
EXCELLENT (80 - 100)

Nominal 50 ms baseline, zero packet drop.

GOOD (60 - 79)

Minor RF jitter or slight link variance.

DEGRADED (40 - 59)

Elevated latency, congestion or packet loss.

CRITICAL (< 40)

Severe latency spike or active cyber attack.
